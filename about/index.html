About the property

Duck world.
Real receipts.

Donald the Lame Duck is an original political-satire comic about public conduct, institutional absurdity, and power pretending the calendar is optional.

What is real?

Every topical strip links to the public reporting that supplied its factual starting point. Dialogue, situations, and duck-world framing are satire and commentary.

What is original?

The characters and artwork belong to this property. It is not affiliated with Disney, a campaign, a public office, or a political organization.
